# Keep Daily calorie target separate from TDEE

When Goal direction (Lose/Maintain/Gain, derived from Goal weight vs. current weight) was added, we considered adjusting TDEE itself by ±500 kcal so the whole app — daily balance, weight-trend prediction, and the Dashboard's remaining-calories figure — would read from one number. We rejected this: TDEE feeds the weight-trend prediction (`docs/business-logic.md` §3), which compares actual calories consumed against actual physiological expenditure to predict real weight change; adjusting it by the user's goal would make that prediction reflect their aspiration instead of their real trajectory, silently wrong the moment they don't hit the target exactly. Instead, TDEE stays the unadjusted physiological baseline (still drives §2's balance math and §3's prediction), and a new derived **Daily calorie target** (TDEE ± 500 per Goal direction) exists purely to drive the Dashboard's "remaining calories" figure and any user-facing recommendation. The two numbers are expected to diverge whenever Goal direction isn't Maintain — that divergence is intentional, not a bug.
